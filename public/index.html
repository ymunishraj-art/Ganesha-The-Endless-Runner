A FEW LITTLE POINTERS

Follow the joy.

1

Find your rhythm.

Use ← → or A D to change lanes. Press SPACE, ↑ or W to jump. ↓ or S brings you down. On touchscreens, swipe sideways to move or up to jump; tap the center to jump, or an edge to change lanes.

2

Gather something sweet.

Each modak earns 100 points. A string of pickups builds your combo up to ×4. Jump over low obstacles or take an open lane around taller ones. There is always a clear path.

3

Let the leaves surprise you.

Find rare Patri for shields, magnets, time-bending blessings and more. Every discovery joins your collection. A soft green trail from Apamarga shows a safe route.

4

Enjoy the whole journey.

Reach the sunset at 30 seconds and the river at 60. Pace quickens every 30 seconds of continuous running. Press ESC or P for a breather. Your best scores, discoveries and achievements are saved on this device.

WITH RESPECT & A LITTLE WONDER

A celebration of beginnings.

Ganesha is honored as the remover of obstacles and the deity of wisdom and new beginnings. This joyful, independent game draws inspiration from Ganesh Chaturthi, the offering of modaks, and the Ekavimshati Patri — twenty-one sacred leaves.

The journey moves from a festive pandal to an evening procession and finally to the river, inspired by Visarjan: a beautiful reminder that every ending holds a beginning.

Regional traditions and plant identifications differ. Our leaf abilities are imaginative game mechanics, not representations of religious teachings or medicinal properties.

Carry the joy. Leave no trace. Celebrate with natural materials, respect local immersion guidance, and help keep our rivers clean.
